## Motivação

O Accelerate mostra que trunk-based development está entre as capacidades ligadas a times de alta performance, mas quase não explica como praticar. Aqui é o lado prático: branches de vida curta, integrar várias vezes ao dia, feature flags e branch by abstraction. Nunca trabalhei assim, então quero entender o fluxo por dentro.

<!-- en -->
## Motivation

Accelerate shows trunk-based development is among the capabilities tied to high-performing teams, but barely explains how to practice it. This is the practical side: short-lived branches, integrating several times a day, feature flags and branch by abstraction. I've never worked this way, so I want to understand the flow from the inside.
